# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. TollMeshStore is dedicated to creating an inclusive environment where everyone feels valued, respected, and empowered to contribute.

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Being respectful**: Treat all community members with respect and dignity
- **Being inclusive**: Welcome people of all backgrounds and perspectives
- **Being constructive**: Provide helpful feedback and suggestions
- **Being collaborative**: Work together to solve problems and improve the project
- **Being patient**: Understand that people have different levels of experience
- **Being professional**: Keep discussions focused on the project and community
- **Listening actively**: Hear and consider different viewpoints
- **Giving credit**: Acknowledge the contributions of others

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **Harassment**: Unwelcome comments, jokes, or advances of a sexual, racial, or otherwise offensive nature
- **Discrimination**: Treating people unfairly based on protected characteristics
- **Intimidation**: Threatening or aggressive behavior
- **Trolling**: Deliberately posting inflammatory or off-topic messages
- **Spam**: Posting repetitive or unsolicited content
- **Doxxing**: Sharing private information without consent
- **Hate speech**: Using slurs or dehumanizing language
- **Sexual harassment**: Unwelcome sexual comments, images, or behavior
- **Abuse**: Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All community spaces (GitHub, Discord, forums, mailing lists, etc.)
- Official project events and conferences
- Interactions between community members
- Representation of the project in public spaces

This Code of Conduct applies equally to all community members, including maintainers and contributors.

## Enforcement

### Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience a violation of this Code of Conduct, please report it by:

1. **Email**: Send a detailed report to conduct@tollmesh.dev
2. **GitHub**: Report through GitHub's abuse reporting system
3. **Direct Message**: Contact a maintainer directly if you feel comfortable

When reporting, please include:

- Description of the incident
- Who was involved
- When and where it occurred
- Any relevant links or screenshots
- Your contact information (optional)

All reports will be treated confidentially and investigated promptly.

### Investigation Process

1. **Receipt**: We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 24 hours
2. **Investigation**: We will investigate the incident thoroughly
3. **Response**: We will take appropriate action based on our findings
4. **Follow-up**: We will follow up with you about the outcome (if appropriate)

### Consequences

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:

- **Warning**: A private warning from maintainers
- **Temporary Ban**: Temporary removal from community spaces (1-7 days)
- **Permanent Ban**: Permanent removal from community spaces
- **Legal Action**: In severe cases, we may pursue legal action

The severity of consequences will be determined based on:

- Nature and severity of the violation
- Pattern of behavior
- Impact on the community
- Willingness to make amends

## Appeals

If you believe a decision was made in error, you may appeal by:

1. Sending a detailed appeal to conduct@tollmesh.dev
2. Explaining why you believe the decision was unfair
3. Providing any additional context or evidence

Appeals will be reviewed by a different maintainer to ensure fairness.
